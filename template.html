How each destination is served

Distances are straight line (haversine) between coordinates. Destination coordinates are approximate town or state capital centres, as the workbook only holds plant and warehouse coordinates. Warehouses are not owned, so cost is variable only (handling plus freight). Sales sources are mapped to reference nodes: Lagos factory to Ota, Lagos to Lagos 1, Ikeja and Ijoko to Lagos 2, Ibadan to South West, Abuja to Central, Port Harcourt and Aba to East, Benin to South Central, Kano to North.
